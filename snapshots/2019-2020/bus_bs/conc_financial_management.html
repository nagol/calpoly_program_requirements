The Financial Management concentration has three required courses, which provide the students with fundamentals of asset valuation, and applies these principles to different securities (bonds, equity, derivatives, and corporate and real assets). The Finance electives enable the students to specialize and deepen their training.

The following are the required and the elective courses in the FM Concentration.

BUS 438Advanced Corporate Finance4
